Lumora Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Lumora is an educational application designed to help students learn, practice questions, explore lessons, and track learning progress.

1. Information We Collect

When a student creates a Lumora account, the app may store:

  • Full name
  • School name
  • Class
  • A Lumora-generated student ID
  • An optional profile picture

Lumora does not require a phone number or precise location to create an account.

2. Learning Information

Lumora stores learning information locally on the device, including completed lessons, attempted questions, learning progress, streak information, and question-practice tracking.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain the student's Lumora profile
  • To provide learning and practice features
  • To display learning progress
  • To track previously encountered practice questions
  • To allow account and data deletion

Lumora does not use this information for advertising purposes.

4. Third-Party Service

Lumora uses Supabase to store online student account information. Information sent to Supabase may include the student's name, school, class, generated student ID, and optional profile-picture information.

5. Photos

Lumora requests access to the device's photo library only when a user chooses to add or change a profile picture. Adding a profile picture is optional.

6. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Lumora does not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, phone numbers, advertising identifiers, or camera data through its app functionality.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application and its service providers. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Students and Children's Privacy

Lumora is an educational application intended for students. We aim to collect only information necessary for account and educational features. Parents or guardians with questions about a student's information may contact us below.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Users can delete their Lumora account from the account section inside the application. The app removes the online account record and locally stored Lumora account and learning-progress information.

Users can also request account deletion without using the app through our external deletion page:

Lumora Account Deletion Page

10. Data Retention

Online account information is retained while it is needed to provide Lumora account functionality. When an account is deleted, the corresponding online account record is intended to be removed.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Lumora's features or data practices change. The updated policy will include a new last-updated date.
